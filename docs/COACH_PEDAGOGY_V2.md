# Coach pedagogy input → Chessy Learning Architecture v2

## Distilled product insight
The gap is not teaching legal movement. The gap is teaching **use**: each piece as a practical tool, then combinations of tools, then constrained positions, then a real game.

The progression proposed by the coach is intentionally lower-complexity first:
- learn to use rook/bishop/queen/knight/pawns/king rather than only naming their legal movement,
- combine piece roles,
- add tactical and strategic discipline,
- use mini-games that remove irrelevant complexity,
- transfer the learned patterns into realistic middlegames,
- teach openings and endings as contextual applications,
- finally coach full games.

## Mini-game examples captured
- king + eight pawns vs king + eight pawns
- rooks with selected pawns vs rooks with selected pawns
- bishops vs bishops
- knights vs knights
- queen vs queen

## 624 exercises
The repository already contains a canonical list of 624 source IDs in book order. Those records currently have no verified semantics. Chessy must treat them as a mapping backlog, not as 624 playable exercises.

The existing five rook exercises are the correct shape for the mapping target because each one has:
- a concrete position,
- a specific task,
- a best move,
- an explanation,
- distractors,
- validation rules,
- concepts trained.

They are a pilot pattern, not proof of source-book correspondence.

## ECO reference supplied by the coach

The ECO chart is retained as a late-stage opening taxonomy, after piece use, coordination and constrained play. It is not the first screen of the curriculum, not a memorization requirement, and not evidence that any of the 624 source exercises belongs to a specific opening family.

## Executable product response

Chessy now exposes one practice entry for every piece domain. The rook route uses the five verified original pilot exercises; the remaining domains launch constrained mini-games with explicit goals and observation cues. The same board surface carries the learning contract so the learner does not lose context between Academy and practice.
